# Demo data: a consulting engagement at Voltaria Energy

**Everything here is fictional.** Voltaria Energy, its documents, its customers, the people in
the meetings and the consulting team were written for this project; any resemblance to a real
company or person is coincidental. A synthetic dataset makes the right answer to every
evaluation question known, which is what allows the system to be measured, and keeps any real
or client data out of a public repository.

The story: a consulting team runs a churn-reduction and data-platform program for Voltaria
Energy, a French electricity retailer, from March to October 2026.

| Folder | Source | Content |
|---|---|---|
| `corpus/meetings` | `meetings` | 10 meeting transcripts (kick-off, workshop, weekly syncs, reviews, 3 steering committees), 5 in French and 5 in English, in Markdown with front matter, WebVTT, SRT and plain text |
| `corpus/policies` | `policies` | Voltaria's policies: remote work, leave, travel and expenses, information security, gifts and ethics |
| `corpus/products` | `products` | Offer catalogue, Fixe 3 ans, Verte, Drive (EV), solar and heat pumps, smart meter FAQ |
| `corpus/support` | `support` | Billing disputes, outages, retention playbook, moving, SLA and escalation matrix |
| `insights/` | | Decisions, action items, risks and summaries extracted from each transcript by `polyrag extract` (cached LLM output, with the model and prompt version that produced it) |

The analytics database (`offers`, `customers`, `monthly_consumption`, `support_tickets`) is
generated by `polyrag ingest` with a fixed seed (`src/polyrag/demo/voltaria_db.py`), together
with the meeting tables (`meetings`, `meeting_participants`, `decisions`, `action_items`,
`risks`). Offer prices in the database match the product documents, and figures quoted in the
meetings (the pilot's 25% save-rate target, heat pumps from EUR 11,500) match the documents.

To use your own material, put transcripts in `meetings/` (`.txt`, `.md`, `.vtt` or `.srt`,
named `YYYY-MM-DD_title.ext` or with a `date:` header) and documents in the other folders, then
set `POLYRAG_CORPUS_DIR`.
