# Voltaria Fixe 3 ans

A residential electricity offer with a price per kWh guaranteed for three years.

## Price guarantee

The price of the kWh excluding taxes is **fixed for 36 months** from the start of the contract. Only changes in taxes and regulated network charges decided by public authorities are passed on to the customer. The current price is **€0.2016 per kWh** including taxes, with a monthly fee of **€12.40** for a 6 kVA meter.

## Eligibility

- Residential customers with a meter between 3 and 36 kVA.
- Available in mainland France.
- A smart meter is not required.

## Termination and switching

There is **no commitment and no termination fee**: the customer can switch supplier or offer at any time, even during the 36-month guarantee period. Switching to another Voltaria offer ends the price guarantee.

## End of the guarantee period

Two months before the end of the 36-month period, the customer receives an email with the new Fixe 3 ans price. Without any action, the contract is renewed for a new 36-month period at that price. The customer can instead choose any other Voltaria offer at no cost.

## Who is it for?

Households who want budget certainty and protection against market price increases. In 2025, customers on Voltaria Fixe 3 ans had the lowest churn rate of all residential offers.
