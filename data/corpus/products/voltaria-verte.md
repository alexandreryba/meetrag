# Voltaria Verte

Voltaria Verte is a residential electricity offer supplying **100% renewable electricity**.

## Renewable origin

For every kWh consumed, Voltaria cancels a **guarantee of origin** issued by a French renewable plant. The 2026 supply mix is **70% hydropower** (Alpine run-of-river plants) and **30% wind power** (onshore wind farms in Occitanie and Hauts-de-France). Guarantees of origin are tracked in the national registry and the yearly certificate is available in the Voltaria app.

## Price

The price is indexed on the regulated sales tariff **plus 3%**: €0.2050 per kWh including taxes and a monthly fee of €12.90 for a 6 kVA meter (prices from 1 August 2026). Price changes follow the regulated tariff and are notified one month in advance.

## Carbon report

Every month, the Voltaria app shows a **carbon report** comparing the customer's electricity footprint with the national average mix, together with personalised tips to reduce consumption.

## Conditions

No commitment and no termination fees. Available to residential customers in mainland France with a meter from 3 to 36 kVA.
