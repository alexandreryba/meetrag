# Smart Meter FAQ

## Who owns and installs the smart meter?

The meter belongs to the distribution network operator (DNO), not to Voltaria. Installation and replacement are **free of charge** for the customer and are scheduled directly by the DNO.

## How are my bills calculated?

With a smart meter, readings are transmitted **daily** by the DNO and bills are based on actual consumption. If no reading is available (communication failure), the bill is **estimated** and automatically corrected on the next actual reading.

## Can I submit my own meter reading?

Yes. Customers can submit a self-reading in the Voltaria app **between the 1st and the 5th of each month**. Self-readings are used when the DNO reading is missing.

## Can I see my hourly consumption?

Yes, after giving consent in the app. Hourly consumption data is then displayed with a one-day delay. Customers can withdraw consent at any time; data access is logged.

## What if I think my meter is faulty?

Contact customer support: the agent opens a **meter ticket** and asks the DNO for a meter check. If the meter is found faulty, consumption is recalculated and any overbilling is refunded.

## Is a smart meter required for every offer?

Only for time-of-use offers such as **Voltaria Drive**, because off-peak and peak consumption must be measured separately.
