# Voltaria Drive

Voltaria Drive is a time-of-use electricity offer designed for households with an electric vehicle.

## Off-peak hours

- **Off-peak hours: every day from 23:00 to 07:00**, at **€0.1350 per kWh**.
- Peak hours (all other hours): **€0.2290 per kWh**.
- Monthly fee: **€14.50** for a 6 kVA meter.

A smart meter is required, since consumption must be measured by time slot.

## Smart charging

The Voltaria app connects to compatible cars and wallboxes and **automatically schedules charging during off-peak hours**. Customers set a target battery level and departure time; the app handles the rest.

## Wallbox pack

Voltaria offers a **7.4 kW wallbox installed by a certified partner for €1,190**, installation included. Voltaria Drive customers receive an **installation credit of €300** on their electricity bill, bringing the net cost to €890. Installation is scheduled within three weeks of the order.

## Expected savings

For an electric car driven **10,000 km per year** and charged mostly off-peak, a household saves on average **€210 per year** compared with Voltaria Essentielle.

## Conditions

No commitment and no termination fees. The installation credit is clawed back pro rata if the customer leaves Voltaria Drive within 12 months.
