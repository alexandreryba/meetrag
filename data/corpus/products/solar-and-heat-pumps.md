# Solar Panels and Heat Pumps

Beyond electricity supply, Voltaria Energy helps households produce their own energy and electrify their heating.

## Solar panel packs

| Pack | Power | Price incl. installation | Expected yearly production (Lyon) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solar 3 | 3 kWp | €7,490 | about 3,750 kWh |
| Solar 6 | 6 kWp | €12,900 | about 7,500 kWh |

Expected production is about **1,250 kWh per kWp per year** in the Lyon area; it varies with roof orientation and region.

### Warranties

- **25-year performance warranty** on panels (at least 80% of the nominal power after 25 years).
- **10-year workmanship warranty** on the installation.
- Inverters are guaranteed for 12 years.

### Selling the surplus

Electricity that is not self-consumed is bought back by Voltaria at **€0.04 per kWh** under a **20-year buyback contract**.

## Heat pumps

Voltaria installs **air-to-water heat pumps** to replace gas or oil boilers, **from €11,500** installed, before public subsidies. **Two years of maintenance** are included. A technical visit is required before the quote.

## Financing

Solar packs and heat pumps can be paid in up to **24 interest-free monthly instalments**, subject to approval by Voltaria's financing partner.
