# Electricity Offers Overview

Voltaria Energy markets five electricity offers. Prices below are valid from **1 August 2026** and include all taxes. Monthly fees are given for a 6 kVA meter (residential offers) and a 12 kVA meter (professional offer).

| Offer | Code | Segment | Pricing | Price per kWh | Monthly fee | Commitment |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Voltaria Essentielle | ESSENTIEL | Residential | Indexed on the regulated tariff, minus 2% | €0.1940 | €11.80 | None |
| Voltaria Verte | VERTE | Residential | Indexed on the regulated tariff, plus 3%; 100% renewable | €0.2050 | €12.90 | None |
| Voltaria Fixe 3 ans | FIXE3 | Residential | Fixed price for 36 months | €0.2016 | €12.40 | None |
| Voltaria Drive | DRIVE | Residential | Time-of-use: peak €0.2290, off-peak €0.1350 | €0.2290 (peak) | €14.50 | None |
| Voltaria Pro Flex | PROFLEX | Professional | Indexed on the wholesale market | €0.1890 | €29.00 | 12 months |

## Choosing an offer

- **Lowest price today:** Voltaria Essentielle, whose price follows the regulated tariff.
- **Budget certainty:** Voltaria Fixe 3 ans, with a kWh price guaranteed for three years.
- **Environmental commitment:** Voltaria Verte, 100% renewable electricity.
- **Electric vehicle owners:** Voltaria Drive, with cheap off-peak hours for charging.
- **Small businesses and professionals:** Voltaria Pro Flex.

## Common conditions

- Residential offers have **no commitment and no termination fees**; customers can leave at any time.
- Monthly payment by direct debit is the default; customers can choose a smoothed monthly payment based on estimated yearly consumption.
- All offers include access to the Voltaria app (consumption tracking, bills, self-readings).
- Price changes on indexed offers are notified **one month in advance** by email.
