# Leave and Time Off Policy

Owner: People & Culture. Applies to employees of Voltaria Energy SAS in France. Effective 1 January 2026.

## Paid annual leave

Every full-time employee earns **25 working days of paid leave** per year (2.08 days per month worked). Leave requests are submitted in PeopleHub at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days.

Employees on the "forfait jours" working-time arrangement (most managers and engineers) also receive **10 RTT days** per year. RTT days cannot be carried over.

## Carry-over

Up to **5 days** of unused paid leave can be carried over to the following year. Carried-over days must be used **before 31 March**; after that date they are lost unless the employee could not take them because of sick leave or parental leave.

## Parental leave

- **Birth parent:** 20 weeks of leave at full salary, including the statutory maternity leave, with Voltaria topping up social-security benefits.
- **Second parent:** 6 weeks of leave at full salary, to be taken within the first six months after the birth or adoption.
- **Adoption:** 16 weeks at full salary for the primary carer.

Parents returning from leave may work part-time (80%) for up to six months with a 90% salary.

## Sick leave

Employees must inform their manager on the first day of absence and send a medical certificate within **48 hours**. Voltaria maintains full salary from the first day of sick leave, for up to 90 days per rolling year.

## Other leave

- **Bereavement:** 5 days for a spouse, partner or child; 3 days for a parent or sibling.
- **Wedding or civil partnership (PACS):** 4 days.
- **Moving house:** 1 day per year.
- **Volunteering:** 1 paid day per year to volunteer for a registered charity.

## Unpaid leave and sabbaticals

After three years of service, employees may request a sabbatical of 6 to 11 months. Requests are answered within 30 days.
