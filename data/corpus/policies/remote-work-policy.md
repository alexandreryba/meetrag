# Remote Work Policy

Owner: People & Culture. Applies to all Voltaria Energy employees on permanent and fixed-term contracts in France. Version 3.2, effective 1 January 2026.

## Purpose

Voltaria supports hybrid work to improve focus time and work-life balance while preserving team cohesion. This policy defines who can work remotely, how often, and with which equipment and allowances.

## Eligibility

Employees become eligible for remote work once they have completed their probation period (three months for most roles). Roles that require physical presence, such as field technicians and the Lyon control-room operators, are excluded. Interns and apprentices may work remotely one day per week with their tutor's agreement.

## Frequency and organisation

- Eligible employees may work remotely up to **3 days per week**.
- Each team defines one **anchor day** per week when everyone is on site.
- Core collaboration hours are **10:00 to 16:00 (Paris time)**; meetings should be scheduled within this window.
- Remote days are declared in PeopleHub by Friday for the following week. Unused remote days cannot be carried over to the next week.

## Equipment and allowances

Voltaria provides a laptop, a 27-inch monitor and a headset to every eligible employee. Employees working remotely at least two days per week receive a **home-office allowance of €45 per month**, paid with the monthly salary. An ergonomic chair can be reimbursed up to **€250 once every five years** on presentation of an invoice through Expensio.

## Working from another country

Employees may work from another country of the EU/EEA or Switzerland for up to **20 working days per calendar year**. Requests must be approved by the manager and HR in PeopleHub at least 10 business days in advance. Working from outside the EU/EEA and Switzerland is not allowed for tax, employment-law and data-security reasons.

## Security while working remotely

Remote work must comply with the Information Security Policy: use the corporate VPN, never work on Restricted data in public places, and lock your screen whenever you leave your device.

## Ending or suspending remote work

A manager may suspend remote work temporarily for business reasons with two weeks' notice, or immediately in case of a security incident involving the employee's device.
