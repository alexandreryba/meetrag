# Travel and Expense Policy

Owner: Finance. Applies to all employees travelling or incurring expenses on behalf of Voltaria Energy. Effective 1 March 2026.

## Booking travel

All business travel is booked through the **Navette** travel platform, which applies the negotiated rates and duty-of-care tracking.

- **Train is mandatory** for journeys of less than 4 hours by train.
- Flights are booked in **economy class**. Business class is allowed only for flights longer than 7 hours and requires approval from a vice-president.
- Car rental is allowed when public transport is not practical; choose a compact or electric vehicle.

## Accommodation

Hotel rates are capped per night, breakfast included:

| City | Cap per night |
|---|---|
| Paris and London | €180 |
| Other cities | €130 |

Exceptions (trade fairs, events) must be pre-approved by the manager in Navette.

## Meals

Meals during business travel are reimbursed at actual cost within these caps: **lunch €22**, **dinner €32**. Alcohol is not reimbursed, except during client dinners approved in advance by a director.

## Mileage

Use of a personal car is reimbursed at **€0.45 per kilometre**, up to 600 km per trip. Tolls and parking are reimbursed on receipt.

## Submitting expenses

- Expenses are submitted in **Expensio** within **30 days** of being incurred.
- A receipt is required for any expense above **€15**.
- Expense reports above **€500** require manager approval; above **€2,000**, approval from a finance controller is also required.
- Late submissions (more than 90 days) are rejected except in documented exceptional circumstances.

## Corporate card

Employees who travel more than four times a year can request a corporate card. Personal use of the corporate card is strictly forbidden and may lead to disciplinary action.
