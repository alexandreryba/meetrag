# Gifts, Hospitality and Business Ethics

Owner: Compliance. Applies to all employees and members of the executive committee. Effective 1 January 2026.

## Principles

Voltaria Energy does not tolerate corruption in any form. No employee may offer, promise, give, request or accept an advantage intended to influence a business decision.

## Accepting gifts and hospitality

- Employees may accept gifts or hospitality worth up to **€75 per giver and per calendar year** without approval.
- Anything above €75 must be **declared in the ethics register within 5 business days** and approved by the manager. Gifts that cannot be approved are returned or donated to charity.
- **Cash, vouchers and gift cards are never accepted**, whatever the amount.
- No gift or hospitality may be accepted from a supplier taking part in an **ongoing tender**.

## Offering gifts

Gifts to customers or partners must be modest, transparent and recorded in the CRM. Gifts to public officials are prohibited.

## Conflicts of interest

Employees declare potential conflicts of interest (family ties with a supplier, outside directorships, investments in a competitor) in the annual declaration and whenever a new situation arises.

## Speaking up

Concerns can be raised with a manager, the Compliance team, or anonymously through the **SpeakUp** whistleblowing platform, available 24/7 in six languages. The ethics committee acknowledges every report within **7 days**. Retaliation against a whistleblower acting in good faith is forbidden.
