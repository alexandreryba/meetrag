# Information Security Policy

Owner: Chief Information Security Officer (CISO). Mandatory for all employees, contractors and interns. Version 5.0, effective 1 February 2026.

## Accounts and authentication

- **Multi-factor authentication (MFA)** is mandatory on every corporate account, including email, VPN and SaaS tools.
- Passwords must be at least **14 characters** long; passphrases stored in the corporate password manager are recommended.
- Passwords are changed only when a compromise is suspected; periodic forced rotation is no longer required.
- Screens lock automatically after **5 minutes** of inactivity.

## Data classification

All information is classified in one of four levels:

1. **Public**: approved for publication (website, brochures).
2. **Internal**: day-to-day business information, shareable with any employee.
3. **Confidential**: sensitive business information (contracts, pricing strategy, roadmaps).
4. **Restricted**: customer personal data, payment data, grid and SCADA data, employee HR records.

Restricted data must stay in approved systems. It must never be sent to personal email addresses or stored on personal devices.

## Removable media and devices

USB storage devices are blocked on corporate laptops. All laptops are encrypted. A lost or stolen device must be reported **immediately** to the Security Operations Centre so it can be wiped remotely.

## Generative AI tools

Public generative-AI tools may only be used with **Public** information. Internal, Confidential and Restricted information may only be processed in the approved **Voltaria AI Workspace**, which keeps data within the EU and does not use it for model training.

## Reporting incidents

Any suspected security incident (phishing, malware, data leak, lost device) must be reported to the Security Operations Centre within **1 hour**, by email to security@voltaria.example or by phone on internal extension **4444** (24/7).

## Awareness and controls

- Phishing simulations are run **every quarter**.
- Security training is mandatory every year and must be completed within 30 days of assignment.
- Managers review their team's access rights every quarter.

Breaches of this policy may lead to disciplinary action.
