# Escalation and SLA Matrix

Owner: Customer Operations. Defines ticket priorities, service levels and the escalation chain.

## Ticket priorities and service levels

| Priority | Definition | First response | Resolution target |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P1** (critical) | Risk to people, medically dependent customer without power, mass incident | **30 minutes** | **4 hours** |
| **P2** (high) | Billing dispute above €200, customer without service, repeated complaint | **1 business day** | **10 business days** |
| **P3** (standard) | Information requests, contract changes, meter questions | **2 business days** | **15 business days** |

Service levels are measured from ticket creation. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding French public holidays.

## Escalation chain

1. **Tier 1** customer-support agent.
2. **Tier 2** expertise teams (Billing Expertise, Contracts, Field Coordination).
3. **Customer Care manager** on duty.
4. **Head of Customer Operations**, for P1 incidents exceeding the resolution target or any media or legal risk.

An open P1 ticket that has had no update for **2 hours** is escalated automatically to the Customer Care manager.

## Customer satisfaction

After a ticket is closed, the customer receives a satisfaction survey (CSAT, from 1 to 5). The target is an **average CSAT of 4.0 or more** per quarter. Any rating of 1 or 2 triggers a callback by a team lead within 3 business days.

## Written complaints

Written complaints are answered within **15 days**. They are tracked separately from tickets and reviewed monthly by the Customer Care management team.
