# Billing Disputes Procedure

Owner: Customer Operations. For customer-support agents (Tier 1) and the Billing Expertise team (Tier 2).

## Scope

A billing dispute is any customer contest of an invoiced amount: unexpected consumption, estimated bill, wrong offer applied, duplicate payment. Disputes are logged as tickets with the category **billing**.

## Handling rules

1. **Acknowledge** the dispute within **1 business day**, by the customer's preferred channel.
2. **Disputes up to €200** are handled by Tier 1 agents. Check meter readings, the offer and price applied, and recent payments.
3. **Disputes above €200** are escalated to the **Billing Expertise team (Tier 2) within 24 hours**, with the analysis already done.
4. The **resolution target is 10 business days** from the date the dispute is received.

## Collection during the investigation

Collection of the **disputed amount is suspended** until the dispute is resolved; the undisputed part of the bill remains due. Reminder letters for the disputed amount are blocked automatically when the ticket is open.

## Goodwill gestures

Agents may grant commercial goodwill gestures within these limits:

| Role | Maximum goodwill gesture |
|---|---|
| Tier 1 agent | €50 |
| Tier 2 team lead | €250 |
| Billing manager | above €250 |

## Refunds

Approved refunds are paid by **bank transfer within 7 business days** after approval, or deducted from the next bill if the customer prefers.

## Unresolved disputes

If the customer is not satisfied with the answer, they may file a written complaint. If the dispute is still unresolved **two months** after the written complaint, the customer can refer the case to the national energy ombudsman. Agents must mention this right in the final answer.
