# Power Outages and Emergencies

Owner: Customer Operations. For all customer-support agents.

## Who is responsible?

Voltaria Energy is an electricity **supplier**: it does not operate the grid. Power outages, network repairs and emergency interventions are handled by the **distribution network operator (DNO)**.

## What the agent does

1. Check the DNO outage map for the customer's address.
2. If an outage is ongoing, give the customer the estimated restoration time and the **DNO emergency number**, available 24/7.
3. Open a ticket with the category **outage**, linked to the customer account.
4. If there is a risk to people (fallen line, burning smell from the meter), tell the customer to stay away and call the emergency services first.

## Medically dependent customers

Customers who depend on electrical medical equipment are flagged **MDC** (medically dependent customer) in the CRM.

- Any outage affecting an MDC customer is a **P1 ticket**.
- The customer must be **called back within 30 minutes**.
- The agent escalates immediately to the DNO priority line and informs the shift supervisor.

## Compensation

Customers affected by an outage lasting **more than 5 hours** due to the network are entitled to a flat-rate compensation paid by the DNO. Voltaria forwards the claim on the customer's behalf; the compensation appears on a later bill.

## Mass incidents

When more than 1,000 customers are affected (storms, major incidents), the shift supervisor activates the mass-incident procedure: a voice message on the hotline, a banner in the app, and proactive SMS to affected customers.
