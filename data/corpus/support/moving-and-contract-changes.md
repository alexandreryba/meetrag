# Moving House and Contract Changes

Owner: Customer Operations. For customer-support agents.

## Moving house

- Customers should notify Voltaria at least **7 days before** moving.
- The contract is **transferred to the new address with the same offer**, at no cost.
- A **meter reading on the moving day** closes the account at the old address; customers can enter it in the app or the DNO reads it remotely.
- If the new home has no active supply point, a commissioning request is sent to the DNO (lead time about 5 business days; the DNO commissioning fee is passed on at cost).
- Tickets are logged with the category **move**.

## Changing offer

Customers can switch to another Voltaria offer **free of charge** from the app or by phone. The new offer starts on the next billing period. Leaving Voltaria Fixe 3 ans ends its price guarantee; leaving Voltaria Drive within 12 months triggers a pro-rata clawback of the wallbox installation credit.

## Changing meter power

Changing the subscribed power (kVA) is carried out by the DNO. The DNO fee (about **€40**) is passed on at cost on the next bill, and the monthly fee is updated accordingly.

## Changing the contract holder

The contract holder can be changed (for example after a separation or a death) on presentation of supporting documents. The new holder signs a new contract; the outstanding balance stays with the former holder or the estate.

## Contract-related tickets

Changes of offer, power or holder are logged with the category **contract**. Standard requests are processed within **3 business days**.
