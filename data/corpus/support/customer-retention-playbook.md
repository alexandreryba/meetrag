# Customer Retention Playbook

Owner: Customer Operations. For agents of the Retention team, who receive every termination request.

## Golden rule

Customers are **free to leave** at any time; residential offers have no termination fees. Never block or delay a termination. The termination takes effect on the date the new supplier switches the meter.

## Step 1: understand the reason

Ask an open question and record a **reason code** in the CRM:

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| PRICE | Customer finds the price too high |
| COMPETITOR_OFFER | Customer received a competing offer |
| SERVICE | Dissatisfaction with support or billing |
| MOVE | Customer is moving (see the moving procedure, often no termination needed) |
| OTHER | Any other reason |

## Step 2: propose a relevant solution

- **PRICE or COMPETITOR_OFFER:** compare the customer's yearly cost with **Voltaria Fixe 3 ans** (price certainty) and **Voltaria Essentielle** (lowest current price). **Never match a competitor's price.**
- **SERVICE:** apologise, solve the underlying ticket first, and offer a callback by a team lead.
- **MOVE:** offer to transfer the contract to the new address at no cost.
- **Voltaria Drive customers:** offer a free smart-charging audit.

## Loyalty gestures

| Customer tenure | Gesture |
|---|---|
| At least 2 years | **One month of subscription fees free** (once every 12 months) |
| At least 5 years | One month free **plus a €30 credit** |

Gestures are only granted when the customer agrees to stay.

## Step 3: record the outcome

Record the outcome (SAVED or LOST) in the CRM. The team's target **save rate is 25%** of termination requests.
